Sports Activities
The college encourages students to participate actively in sports and physical activities as an important part of overall personality development. Sports help students build discipline, teamwork, leadership qualities, confidence, and a healthy lifestyle. The college provides opportunities for students to participate in various indoor and outdoor games and represent the institution at different levels.
Outdoor Sports Activities
The college promotes participation in a variety of outdoor sports, including:
Cricket – Students are encouraged to develop teamwork, coordination, and competitive spirit through cricket activities.
Football – Football training and matches help students improve fitness, strategy, and team coordination.
Volleyball – Students participate in volleyball activities to enhance agility, communication, and sportsmanship.
Basketball – Basketball provides opportunities for students to improve physical endurance and teamwork.
Indoor Sports Activities
The college also encourages students to participate in indoor games, including:
Badminton – Helps students improve concentration, speed, and coordination.
Table Tennis – Provides opportunities to develop quick reflexes and competitive skills.
Chess – Encourages strategic thinking, planning, and mental ability.
Carrom – Promotes focus, precision, and recreational participation.
Sports Events and Competitions
The college organizes annual sports events, competitions, and tournaments where students can showcase their talent and sports skills. Students are encouraged to participate in inter-college, university-level, and other recognized competitions.
Sports Facilities
The college provides necessary sports facilities and guidance through dedicated sports activities. Students receive support from the physical education department for training, practice sessions, and participation in various events.
Achievements and Recognition
Students who perform exceptionally well in sports are encouraged and recognized for their achievements. The college motivates talented athletes to represent the institution at district, state, and university levels.
