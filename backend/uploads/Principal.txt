Principal's Message


Dear Students, Faculty, and Visitors,

It is with immense pleasure that I welcome you to the official website of Government College for Women, M.A. Road, constituent college, Cluster University, Srinagar. As an institution dedicated to empowering women through education, we strive to foster an environment where academic excellence, innovation, and holistic development converge. Our college stands as a beacon of learning, offering a range of programs designed to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to succeed in today's rapidly changing world.

Aligned with the vision of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, we are committed to providing an inclusive, interdisciplinary, and flexible learning experience. The NEP emphasizes creativity, critical thinking, and experiential learning—values that are at the heart of our academic approach. We believe in nurturing not only intellectual growth but also the personal and professional development of our students, empowering them to make meaningful contributions to society.

At Government College for Women, M.A.Road, we offer a diverse array of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across science, humanities and technology. In addition to our academic programs, we provide opportunities for research, extracurricular activities, and community engagement. Our campus is equipped with modern facilities, a resourceful library, and technology-driven learning spaces to support both students and faculty in their academic endeavors.

I invite you to learn more about our courses, faculty, and the many opportunities we offer to support your educational journey. Whether you are considering joining us as a new student or are already part of our vibrant community, we are committed to helping you reach your fullest potential.

Thank you for visiting, and I look forward to the continued growth and success of this institution.

Warm regards,

Prof.(Dr.) Yasmeen Farooq
