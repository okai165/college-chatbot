Government College for Women, M.A. Road, Srinagar, a constituent college of Cluster University Srinagar, was established in 1950 and is recognized as one of the premier institutions for women's higher education in Jammu and Kashmir. Situated in the heart of Srinagar, the college has earned a distinguished reputation for academic excellence, holistic student development, and its enduring contribution towards women's empowerment and social transformation in the Union Territory.

The college campus spreads across 8.5 acres, of which nearly 4.23 acres comprise lush green and open spaces including playgrounds, botanical gardens, an eco-park, hostel grounds, and landscaped areas that provide a serene and student-friendly learning environment.

The institution is supported by a dedicated workforce of more than 150 faculty members and staff who collectively uphold high standards in academics, administration, mentoring, research, and campus life.

Committed to the vision of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, the college offers a wide range of programmes under the Four-Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUP), in addition to integrated and postgraduate programmes across diverse disciplines. The institution also offers innovative skill-based and vocational courses to enhance employability and industry readiness.

The college boasts modern infrastructure equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories, smart classrooms, ICT-enabled teaching-learning facilities, a well-stocked library, browsing and learning centres, seminar halls, sports facilities, and advanced digital infrastructure.

The institution places strong emphasis on research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and capacity building. Faculty members actively engage in research publications, faculty development programmes, and collaborative academic initiatives. The college has signed various Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) with reputed institutions and organizations to facilitate academic exchange, internships, skill enhancement, and research collaboration.

In alignment with contemporary educational and governance reforms, the college actively promotes digital governance, student-centric services, environmental sustainability, inclusivity, and quality assurance practices through various institutional mechanisms and committees.
